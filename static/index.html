EVIDENCE BEFORE THE EDIT

A good story survives
the fact check.

01

Measure in code

Lap averages and differences are calculated deterministically. The language model selects the comparison and organizes the editorial angle.

02

Show what was excluded

Missing data, invalid times, pit entry and out-laps, and laps overlapping caution messages are listed. Caution intervals are not fully reconstructed.

03

Keep cause separate

A lap-time difference cannot prove that a tyre choice or pit stop caused a result. Causal claims are held as unestablished.

04

Verify the delivery

Drive uploads are checked, Notion pages are retrieved, and Discord returns a message receipt. Failed runs retain their completed steps.
